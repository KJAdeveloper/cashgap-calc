Invoice late fees explained

Late payment costs you twice: what the contract allows you to charge, and what that cash could have earned (or paid down) while you waited.

Contract late fees

Only apply if your agreement says so — and local law allows. Percent-per-month fees are often pro-rated by days late after any grace period. Flat fees may be one-time or monthly.

Opportunity cash-gap

Even with $0 collectible fees, money stuck in AR isn’t earning your target yield. That’s the economic drag CashGap adds to the picture.
