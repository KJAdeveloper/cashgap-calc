Payment reminder sequence for freelancers

Most late invoices aren’t malice — they’re inbox noise. A short, polite sequence cuts DSO without a fight.

Day 0 — Invoice + due date clear

Send the invoice the day work’s approved. Subject: “Invoice #[#] — due [date]”. Attach PDF + payment link. Confirm “net-15” or “net-30” in one line.

Day +3 after due — Soft nudge

“Quick check — invoice #[#] for $[amount] was due [date]. Here’s the link again: [pay]. Happy to resend a W-9 or PO if finance needs it.”

Day +10 — Firm + options

“Following up on invoice #[#] (now [X] days late). Can you confirm payment date this week? If cash is tight, tell me a date I can plan around.” Mention late-fee terms only if they’re already in the contract.

Automate the boring part

Manual chase doesn’t scale. Invoicing tools with auto-reminders (Wave) and pay-links (Melio) keep the sequence running while you work.
